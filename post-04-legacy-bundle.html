The Stories Only Get Told Once

By Sprout & Season · SparkPrint Studio

Some stories only get told once — usually at the kitchen table, and usually when nobody is writing them down. A grandparent starts talking about the house they grew up in, or the year everything was hard, and the whole table goes quiet. Then the moment passes, and the story goes back to living in one person's memory.

That's why we put these two books together.

The journal: a whole life, in their own words

Grandparent Legacy Memory Journal — $9.99 (before the sale discount), 55 pages. It asks a grandparent the questions families always mean to ask: where they grew up, who they loved, what work they did, what the hard years taught them — 122 guided questions in all, plus a family tree to fill in. The print is large and the pages are unhurried, so it can be done a little at a time, in their own handwriting.

The recipe book: the dish, and the memory with it

Family Recipe Keepsake Book — $8.99 (before the sale discount), 50 pages with room for 32 recipes. Every recipe page has a box called "The Memory With This Dish" — because the recipe is only half of it. The other half is who taught it to them, and when your family ate it, and why it mattered. Those are the parts that disappear first.

The two together: the Legacy Bundle

The Legacy Bundle — $15.99 for both books together. Bought separately they come to $18.98, so the bundle saves $2.99. Print them at home, put them in a binder, and let them fill the pages over the winter. It makes a Christmas gift that doesn't get used up — it gets filled up, and handed down.

One download, a home printer, and a quiet afternoon of questions. Years from now, nobody will wish they had fewer of these stories written down.
